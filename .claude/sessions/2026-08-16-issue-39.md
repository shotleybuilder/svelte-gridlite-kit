# Session: Svelte 5 / SvelteKit 2 Compatibility (#39) (CLOSED)

## Problem

GridLite's `.svelte` components use Svelte 4 patterns (`export let`, `$$slots`, `on:event` dispatching) that may not work under Svelte 5. Consumer projects like sertantai-compliance are stuck on Svelte 4 patterns, generating false-positive svelte-check warnings. Svelte 5 migration requires understanding what breaks and what needs rewriting.

A previous attempt (~mid-2026) was blocked by dependency issues — likely TanStack DB not supporting Svelte 5 at that time. Many deps have since released updates, so this audit must also check whether the blockers have been resolved.

## Strategy

**Option C with a twist:** Cut a `svelte-4` branch from current main as a frozen snapshot (no active maintenance). Then migrate main to full Svelte 5 runes. The `svelte-4` branch is an insurance policy — consumers who aren't ready can pin to it. Delete when no longer needed.
